The Rapid Rise of Resale: Exploring Asia's Secondhand Boom

Published on 2025-06-02

The growth of secondhand platforms in Asia (Mercari, Carousell, Danggeun Market, Xianyu) reflects a cultural shift toward sustainable, trust-based commerce.

Key Drivers

  • Social media-driven thrift culture (TikTok, Instagram).
  • Shift to C2C (Consumer to Consumer) with minimal intermediaries.
  • Hyperlocal trust models (e.g., Danggeun Market in South Korea).

Platform UX Strategies

Mercari (Japan): Seamless nationwide shipping and simple UI.

Carousell (Singapore): Chat-first UX and casual bartering.

Danggeun Market (South Korea): Location-based matching and community-driven design.

Future Outlook

Rise of circular commerce infrastructure, B2B integration, and cross-border resale innovation enabled by AI and fraud prevention tools.
