[Case Study] Analyzing FTUE in RPG Game A and Key Insights for Story-Driven RPG Design

Published on 2025-04-03

FTUE is critical; the first 30 minutes decide whether a player stays.

Research Findings (RPG Game A)

  • Confusion: Lack of tutorials for basic actions (breaking rocks, deactivating traps).
  • Frustration: No skip or speed-up options for dialogue, annoying experienced players.
  • Complexity: Poorly explained equipment systems.

Design Suggestions

  1. Learn Through Story: Integrate controls naturally into the narrative.
  2. Reinforcement: Use lightweight, repeatable practice moments.
  3. Flexibility: Offer "Quick Start" vs. "Detailed Guide" options.
  4. Minimal UI: Use contextual highlights instead of intrusive pop-ups.
