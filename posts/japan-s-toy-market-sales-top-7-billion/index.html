Japan's toy market sales top $7 billion!

Published on 2024-08-08

Japan's toy market surpassed 1 trillion yen ($7 billion) in 2023, driven by strong demand for anime character goods and capsule toys (gachapon). With an aging society and declining birth rates, manufacturers are focusing on adolescents and adults to drive growth.

Gachapon machines are a unique aspect of the market, offering random prizes that attract collectors. Popular items among adults include "Fuchico on the Cup" figurines and miniature furniture. The character business market is tied closely to manga, anime, and video game cycles.
