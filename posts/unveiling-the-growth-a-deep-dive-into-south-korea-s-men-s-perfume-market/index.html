Unveiling the Growth: A Deep Dive into South Korea's Men's Perfume Market

Published on 2024-08-13

The South Korean men's perfume market saw a 15% increase in the first half of 2024, reaching 34 billion won. Cultural shifts where men increasingly embrace grooming and personal care are driving this trend.

The growth of e-commerce has also been a significant factor, with the online segment projected to reach $601.3 million by 2024. As the market matures, there is a growing demand for niche and bespoke fragrances from brands like BYREDO and Diptyque that offer gender-neutral and innovative scents.
