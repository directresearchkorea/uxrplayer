## **DEEP PERSONA**
**Understanding who plays your game and why.**

Deep Persona research goes beyond demographics. We conduct qualitative player research to uncover:
- Gamer motivations
- Deep-seated player behavior
- Core gameplay experiences

Understand the *why* behind player choices to build better games.

## **BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS**
**Multimodal emotion tracking for games.**

We capture players' emotional responses in real-time by integrating physiological data with gameplay analysis:
- Facial Expression Analysis
- Eye Tracking
- Galvanic Skin Response (GSR) & HRV
- Voice Emotion Recognition

Identify moments of excitement, tension, confusion, or frustration.

## **EYE-TRACKING**
**Visual attention and cognitive load.**

See the game through the players' eyes. Our advanced eye-tracking research reveals:
- Visual attention distribution across the UI
- Where players look during high-intensity moments
- Cognitive load and usability friction points

Ensure your UI and essential game elements are effectively communicating with players.
