Economic slowdowns and rising youth unemployment in China have dampened the once-voracious appetite for luxury goods. Mid-tier luxury brands are struggling as they resort to heavy discounting on platforms like Tmall Luxury Pavilion to attract cautious shoppers.

Top-tier labels like Louis Vuitton and Chanel have managed to maintain their position, but others face the risk of eroding brand equity through aggressive price cuts. The emerging phenomenon of "luxury shame" is also leading to reduced footfall in physical stores as consumers avoid ostentatious purchases.
