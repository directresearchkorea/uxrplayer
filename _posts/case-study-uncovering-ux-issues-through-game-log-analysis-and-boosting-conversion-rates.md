Company A used log analysis to address rising churn and low purchase conversion.

## Key Findings

### 1. Interaction Logs
Uncovered that a complex main lobby UI and poorly located guild signup buttons caused early churn.

### 2. Gameplay Logs
Identified mid-level matchmaking delays and a steep difficulty curve that discouraged new players.

### 3. Improvements
- Simplified lobby UI and emphasized essential buttons.
- Introduced AI support to shorten matchmaking times.
- Smoothed difficulty transitions and added tutorial guidance.

## Results

Purchase frequency increased by 15%, and conversion rates for new users improved from 5% to 8%.
