## Demographics

117 participants, mostly male (87), average age 26, top 40% tier players.

## Device Usage

- PC only: 58%
- PC + Mobile: 20%
- Mobile only: 15%

## Top FPS Games

1. **Overwatch 2 (24%)**: Praised for strategic gameplay and unique character skills.
2. **PUBG PC (21%)**: Realistic survival competition and deep weapon mechanics.
3. **Valorant (9%)**: Stylish visuals and tactical character-based play.
4. **Sudden Attack (8%)**: Beginner-friendly and intuitive.

## Conclusion

Simple controls and diverse content are the primary drivers for game selection among young adults in the Korean market.
